Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-16

This privacy policy applies to a set of personal-use automation scripts ("the Tools") that access Google Calendar and Gmail data via the Google APIs on behalf of a single account owner (myself). The Tools are not distributed, sold, or made available to any third party.

What data is accessed

The Tools read Google Calendar events and send Gmail messages using OAuth 2.0 credentials authorized directly by the account owner. No data is transmitted to, stored by, or shared with any external party, service, or company. All processing happens locally on the account owner's own machine.

Data retention

Calendar event identifiers used to avoid duplicate processing are stored locally in a plain-text file on the account owner's machine and are never uploaded anywhere.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. There are no third parties involved in this project.
